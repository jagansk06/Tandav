Fake second device

Plants a real sync bundle in Drive as TANDAV-WEB1, to prove the phone can receive and merge — not just send.

Clean up when you are done, in this order: press Remove the fake device below, then Forget the other device on the phone under Settings → Device & Sync. The phone remembers its partner by exact id, so if you skip the second step it will keep waiting for TANDAV-WEB1 and refuse the real second phone.
